The House of Kel LLC

Imperio Holdings Group

The House of Kel LLC (THOK) is a veteran-owned holding company and parent organization of the Imperio ecosystem, including Imperio Talent Solutions, Imperio Software, Imperio Supply, and Imperio digital ventures.

THOK specializes in government contracting, SDVOSB procurement, staffing and recruiting solutions, supply chain distribution, and proprietary software systems designed to support federal, state, and commercial operations.

Headquartered in the United States, The House of Kel LLC operates across multiple industries including defense logistics, workforce solutions, SaaS development, and enterprise procurement systems.

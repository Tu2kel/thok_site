ClipDesk · The House of Kel

Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026

ClipDesk is a private content-discovery, editing, and publishing application operated by The House of Kel LLC. This page describes how information is handled when the application connects to third-party services, including YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X. Publishing this policy does not make the ClipDesk application publicly accessible: it has no public sign-up and is used only by people we authorize, to publish to accounts we own or manage.

1. Information processed

Depending on the features in use and the permissions granted, ClipDesk processes:

  • Connected account details. For Facebook, the Page's ID and name and, where one is linked, the ID of the Page's Instagram business account. For YouTube, TikTok and X, the details the platform returns when the account is connected.
  • Authorization tokens. The access token or refresh token each platform issues, so ClipDesk can publish to that account.
  • Media and publishing data. Videos, audio, images, scripts, captions, descriptions and a record of where each video was posted.
  • Public source metadata. Titles, descriptions, credits and captions of public footage (for example DVIDS, NASA and the Internet Archive) and public YouTube video statistics.
  • Operational data. Job status and error messages used to troubleshoot publishing.

ClipDesk does not read messages, comments, followers, insights or viewing history from any connected account, and collects no information from people who view our published content.

2. How information is used

To retrieve authorized content, prepare media, manage connections, and publish to accounts specifically selected by the authorized operator, and for security and troubleshooting. We do not sell information, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

The public does not need a ClipDesk account to view content we publish on any social network.

3. Social platform connections

Facebook and Instagram. ClipDesk requests permission to list the Pages the operator manages, publish posts to the connected Page, and publish content to its linked Instagram business account. Use of Facebook and Instagram data is subject to the Meta Platform Terms and policies.

YouTube. ClipDesk requests only the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos to the connected channel. ClipDesk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ClipDesk uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok. ClipDesk requests basic profile information and permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account.

X. ClipDesk posts using API credentials the operator configures for our own X account.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information is sent to the platform selected for publication and to the service providers ClipDesk relies on, each receiving only what its service needs:

  • MongoDB Atlas — database hosting.
  • ElevenLabs — voice synthesis and transcripts.
  • WaveSpeed — lip-synced presenter video.

Once published, posts and their metadata are governed by the destination platform's privacy terms.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Credentials are kept out of source code and stored in an access-controlled database. Whenever ClipDesk is reachable from the internet it requires a password, except for the rendered media files that processing providers and platforms must download, which are reachable only by their direct link. A platform's tokens are deleted from ClipDesk's database when that account is disconnected in ClipDesk. Facebook and Instagram share one Meta Page token, which is deleted once both are disconnected. Production files and publishing records are kept until the operator deletes them, or sooner if deletion is requested as described below.

6. Disconnecting accounts and requesting deletion

An account holder can revoke ClipDesk's access at any time in the platform's connected-app settings, for example Google account permissions, Facebook's Business Integrations settings, or TikTok's Manage app permissions. To request removal of information held by ClipDesk, email support@ifedlog.com with the subject ClipDesk Data Deletion Request and identify the connected account or data involved. Revoking access does not remove content already published to a platform; such content must be removed on that platform.

7. Children's privacy

ClipDesk is an internal production tool, is not intended for use by children, and does not solicit personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

The effective date at the top of this page is revised whenever this policy changes.

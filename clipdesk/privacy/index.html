Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

ClipDesk is an internal production and publishing tool operated by LEVRAM Studios, a division of The House of Kel LLC ("we"). It is used only by our own staff to publish to our own social media accounts. This policy explains what data ClipDesk handles and why.

Data we handle

  • Connected account tokens. When we connect one of our own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or X accounts, the platform gives ClipDesk an access token and, where offered, a refresh token. We store these in our private database so ClipDesk can upload videos to that account.
  • Basic account details. The connected account's name or handle and ID, so ClipDesk can show which account a channel posts to.
  • Our own production files. The videos, audio, images and scripts our staff create in ClipDesk, plus a record of where each video was posted.
  • Public footage metadata. Titles, descriptions, credits and captions of public footage from sources such as DVIDS, NASA and the Internet Archive, and public YouTube video statistics.

ClipDesk has no public sign-up and collects no data from viewers of our videos.

How we use it

Only to produce our videos and upload them to the accounts we connected. We do not sell, rent or share this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Service providers we rely on to run ClipDesk (database hosting, voice synthesis and video processing) receive only what they need to perform that service.

Google and YouTube data

ClipDesk requests the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos to the YouTube channel we connect. It does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or viewing history. ClipDesk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ClipDesk uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected in ClipDesk, at which point they are deleted. You can also revoke access at any time from the platform itself, for example Google account permissions, Facebook's Business Integrations settings, or TikTok's Manage app permissions. To ask us to delete any data ClipDesk holds about an account, email info@imperiovita.co and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Security

Credentials are kept out of source code, the database is access-controlled, and ClipDesk requires a password whenever it is reachable from the internet.

Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes.

ClipDesk

LEVRAM Studios' in-house commentary desk. It finds footage worth a take, puts the studio's presenter on screen around the clips, and publishes the finished Short to the studio's own channels.

FindStudioLibraryPublish

What it does

  • Find — searches public footage sources, including the Defense Visual Information Distribution Service (DVIDS), NASA and the Internet Archive, and ranks YouTube videos the studio is considering commenting on.
  • Studio — the producer writes the commentary, marks short excerpts of the source to cut in, and renders a vertical video with captions.
  • Library — keeps each finished video and a record of where it was posted.
  • Publish — uploads finished videos to LEVRAM Studios' own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X accounts, once the studio has connected them.

How it treats source material

  • Commentary is the product: excerpts are capped at half of every video's runtime.
  • Every excerpt is credited, and the credit is carried into the published description.
  • DVIDS material is used under its public-release terms with attribution to DVIDS and the originating unit. ClipDesk does not imply endorsement by the Department of Defense.
  • ClipDesk never downloads videos from YouTube.

Who uses it

ClipDesk is an internal tool operated by LEVRAM Studios, a division of The House of Kel LLC, for its own channels. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

# AAD Target List — Federal Supply-Base Project

**AAD / AD = Approved Authorized Distributor.** This replaces the retired ~2,500-vendor
federal mass-blast (blast-and-pray POC failed, killed 2026-07-15). The new federal
supply base is a **curated, FSC-routed set of authorized distributors + direct
manufacturer channels**, worked by targeted outreach — not a spray.

## The two lanes

1. **Buy-from-AAD** — large authorized aerospace/mil-spec distributors that open a small
   reseller account and sell IFL **traceable product (mfr C of C + lot trace) at reseller
   pricing**, ideally drop-ship **FOB origin** to a gov depot. Covers Unrestricted DIBBS
   demand (any spec-compliant source) *and* restricted/QPL items when the AAD stocks the
   approved manufacturer's product with certs.
2. **Get-authorized-direct** — manufacturers with repeat DIBBS demand that use dealer/
   distributor channels and might authorize IFL directly as a government reseller. Higher
   effort, higher moat.

## Ground rules
- **Federal mass-blast stays dead.** This list is the federal supply side now.
- **SAM-derived cold wholesalers → ESBD (State side)** unless individually proven as a real AAD.
- Vetting pitch below separates a real AAD from another retail / direct-DLA supplier.

## Vetting pitch — ask every distributor (NOT just "can I open an account?")

> Imperio Federal Logistics is an SDVOSB government reseller supplying DLA and other public
> agencies. We are seeking an authorized distribution partner that permits resale to federal
> customers.
>
> Do you support small reseller accounts, provide manufacturer C of C and full traceability,
> offer distributor/reseller pricing, permit direct shipment to government depots, and quote
> exact DIBBS part-number requirements?
>
> We are not requesting exclusivity or credit terms initially and can purchase by card or
> prepaid while establishing the relationship.

**The five yes/no answers that qualify a real AAD:** small-reseller accounts · mfr C of C +
traceability · distributor/reseller pricing · drop-ship to gov depot · quotes exact DIBBS P/Ns.

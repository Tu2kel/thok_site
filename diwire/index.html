How it works

Watch it. Wire it. Test it.

Every circuit runs from the panel to the last device, with the code reason for each step.

Watch

See the whole job built, step by step

Tell DiWire what you're wiring. It animates the build from the home run at the panel to the last wire nut, and explains every term as it goes.

  • Wire gauge and breaker size checked against the 60 °C column
  • Box fill worked out for every box
  • Common mistakes shown so you can spot them
Wire

Then do it yourself, with no guide marks

Drag every wire end where you think it goes. Land the home run on the right breaker, then turn it on. Real-world mode stays quiet until power-on. Coach mode points out mistakes as you make them.

  • Drag and drop on phone, tablet or computer
  • Graded at the panel, AFCI and GFCI included
  • Pass it and get a job card to take to the real box
Test

Troubleshoot with a tester pen

Sweep a non-contact tester over any wire the way you would on the job. It beeps on the hot conductor and stays silent on the neutral, so you learn what "dead" really means.

  • Live-dead-live habit built into every job
  • Switch the circuit on and off and see what changes
  • Hot and neutral current drawn in color

Questions

Before you start

Which code does DiWire follow?

The 2023 National Electrical Code. Cities and states adopt the NEC on their own schedule and add amendments, so confirm the edition your inspector enforces.

Do I still need a permit?

Often, yes. Many places require a permit and inspection for new circuits, even for homeowners. Call your local building department before you start.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Watch, wire and test all work on phone, tablet and computer. Drag-and-drop wiring works with touch.

What if I'm brand new to this?

Start with the learning path. It begins at a single switch and a single outlet, and every term has a plain-English explanation one tap away.

What do I get with Pick 3?

You choose any 3 circuits and keep them for good, with Try-it, the tester and job cards. Need more later? Buy another Pick 3 or upgrade to Pro.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, anytime. You keep access until the end of the year you paid for.
